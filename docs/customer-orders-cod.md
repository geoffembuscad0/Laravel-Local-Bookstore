# Customer Orders and Metro Manila COD

Customer checkout is limited to Metro Manila (including Pateros) and uses Cash on Delivery only. Checkout collects a Philippine mobile number and local delivery address details including barangay, city/municipality, four-digit postal code, and an optional landmark. The selected delivery address is saved with each order; the account profile is not changed by checkout.

Customer order states are: **Order received** (`pending`), **Preparing your order** (`processing`), **Out for delivery** (`out_for_delivery`), **Delivered** (`delivered`), and **Cancelled** (`cancelled`). Customers can cancel an order only while it is pending. Orders are always unpaid at placement for COD; no online payment is taken. Status changes beyond customer cancellation require bookstore staff handling.
